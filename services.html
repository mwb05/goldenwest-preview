What we do

Six things, done properly

What each service actually covers, and when it is worth booking one.

01

Tires

Purchase and installation, repair, alignment, rotation and balancing.

When to come in. Come in when you see uneven wear, feel a vibration at speed, or the tread is down to the wear bars.

  • New tires from the brands we stock, fitted and balanced
  • Puncture and sidewall repair where the tire can be saved safely
  • Four-wheel alignment when the car pulls or the wear is uneven
  • Rotation every 5,000–7,500 miles to even out tread wear
02

Oil, Lube and Filters

Regular oil changes and lubricant replacement to keep the engine running strong.

When to come in. Follow the interval in your owner’s manual — we will tell you what it says for your vehicle.

  • Conventional, synthetic blend and full synthetic
  • Oil filter replaced with every change
  • Chassis lubrication where the vehicle has fittings
  • Engine and cabin air filters checked and replaced
03

Brakes

Inspection, pad and rotor replacement, and fluid service.

When to come in. Come in for squealing, grinding, a soft pedal, or a car that pulls to one side when stopping.

  • Full brake inspection, front and rear
  • Pad replacement and rotor resurfacing or replacement
  • Brake fluid flush and replacement
  • Caliper and hardware checks
04

Batteries

Testing, replacement and maintenance for reliable starting.

When to come in. Most batteries last three to five years. Slow cranking on a cold morning is the first sign.

  • Charging system and battery load testing
  • Replacement batteries fitted the same visit
  • Terminal cleaning and corrosion removal
  • Alternator and starter checks
05

Routine Services and Fluids

Coolant, brake fluid and transmission fluid inspected and replaced.

When to come in. The services that keep a car out of the repair shop rather than in it.

  • Coolant and radiator service
  • Transmission fluid inspection and replacement
  • Power steering and differential fluids
  • Belts, hoses and wiper blades
06

Suspension and Front End

Alignment and front-end work for a safe, comfortable ride.

When to come in. Come in for a bouncy ride, a wandering steering wheel, or tires wearing out on one edge.

  • Shocks and struts
  • Ball joints, tie rods and control arms
  • Front-end alignment
  • Steering and suspension inspection
