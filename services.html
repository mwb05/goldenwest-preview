Our Services

Tire Services and Repair

Big O Tires offers a full range of tire services including tire purchase and installation, repair, alignment, pressure checks, rotation, balancing, and snow tire changeovers, ensuring your tires perform optimally and safely.

Oil Change, Lube, and Filters

Regular oil changes and necessary lubricant replacements are performed to maintain your vehicle's performance and longevity.

Routine Auto Services and Fluids

Essential vehicle fluids like coolant, brake fluid, and transmission fluid are inspected and replaced to ensure smooth and efficient operation.

Car Batteries

Battery testing, replacement, and maintenance services are provided to ensure reliable vehicle starting and proper function.

Brake Repair

Comprehensive brake services, including inspections, pad and rotor replacements, and fluid changes, maintain the effectiveness of your braking system.

Suspension and Front End Services

Issues related to suspension and front-end alignment are handled to ensure a comfortable and safe driving experience.
