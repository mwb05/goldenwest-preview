Open Roles

Careers

01

Mechanic

The mechanic performs service and installation activities associated with Big O maintenance and repair services in a safe, accurate, and timely manner. Responsibilities include brake work, suspension work, exhaust work, under-hood services, and performing safety inspections.

02

Salesperson

The salesperson educates guests on vehicle manufacturer’s recommendations, explaining service benefits and offering services while providing superior guest service. Responsibilities include greeting guests and entering guest information into the computer system.

03

Manager in Training

The Manager in Training (MIT) is responsible for overseeing store operations, personnel, facilities, guest service, safety, sales, and profitability at a Big O location. Responsibilities include managing staff, ensuring guest satisfaction, and maintaining cleanliness.

04

Tire Technician

The Tire Technician handles service and installation activities related to Big O automotive maintenance and tire services safely and accurately. Duties include mounting, dismounting, balancing, repairing, and inspecting tires, along with performing alignments.

05

Service Manager

The Service Manager assists the General Manager in the daily operation of a Big O Service Center, assuming full operational control in their absence. Responsibilities include leading and managing staff, ensuring completion of services, and maintaining guest vehicles.

06

General Manager

The Tire Center Manager oversees all activities within their Big O location, including store operations, personnel management, guest service, safety, sales, and profitability. Responsibilities include managing staff, ensuring guest satisfaction, and controlling costs.
