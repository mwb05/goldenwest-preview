Big O Tires Overview

The Big OTires Story

The Big O Tires story begins in 1962, amid America’s burgeoning love affair with the automobile and the exploding market for replacement tires. Independent tire dealers struggled to compete against low prices from major tire manufacturers’ own stores, leading a group of them to band together.

This cooperative evolved into a full-fledged franchise, leveraging collective power to benefit independent dealerships and their customers, eventually becoming North America’s largest retail tire franchisor with over 480 locations in 20 states.
